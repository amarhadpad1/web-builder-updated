2. Overall feel

Luxury and Classic = sharp corners. Friendly = very round. Modern = clean and simple.

3. Layout style

Choose how the website is arranged. Each layout looks different, so client websites do not all look the same. A new client starts with a random layout. The preview and the AI prompt follow your choice.

4. Colours

Pick a ready-made palette. Each one has a main colour, accent, background, text and muted surface colour. Use about 60% background, 30% secondary surfaces and 10% accent.

Colours for different types of websites (guide)
Website or brandRecommended colours
Creative or branding agencyBlack, cream, orange
Premium web design agencyBlack, ivory, gold
AI or technology startupNavy, electric blue, white
SaaS or web applicationSlate, blue, light grey
E-commerce storeWhite, charcoal, one strong accent
Finance or businessNavy, forest green, white
Healthcare or wellnessTeal, sage, white
Fashion or luxuryBlack, burgundy, champagne
Personal portfolioMonochrome with one accent
Youthful startupViolet, coral, off-white

These are common design conventions, not universal preferences. The audience, industry and quality of the design matter more than any one colour.
